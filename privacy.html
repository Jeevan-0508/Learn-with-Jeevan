What this static site does

The site does not intentionally use analytics, advertising cookies, a database or a form backend. The learning interactions run in the browser.

Inquiry form

When a visitor submits the inquiry form, the browser opens the visitor's own email application with a prepared message addressed to jeevansiddhabhaktula@gmail.com. Nothing is sent to this website's server. If the visitor sends the email, the message and its contents are processed by the email providers involved.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages may process technical access data when serving the website. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and project repositories take visitors to third-party services with their own privacy information.

Owner completion

Before public operation, add the responsible provider, purposes, legal bases, retention, recipients, data-subject rights and contact details required for the actual setup. Do not include unnecessary information about children in an inquiry.
